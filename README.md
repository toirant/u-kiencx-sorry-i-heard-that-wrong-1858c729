Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Sorry, I Heard That Wrong"](https://make.fun/c/u-kiencx-sorry-i-heard-that-wrong-1858c729): the funniest one-line apology a smart speaker gives after mishearing its owner.

## The entry

> Sorry — you said 'order pizza,' I heard 'call your ex.' She's on her way. The pizza is too.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, no dependencies) writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt`. The speaker misheard "order pizza" as "call your ex" and reports the disaster in a calm, helpful tone — then undercuts it: both the ex and the pizza are on their way, and it's proud of the one it got right.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
